Who works on what, and how full is everyone's plate?

Team capacity puts your team's research projects, teaching, supervision and meetings in one place: who is on what, with involvement, tasks, milestones and deadlines. A shared picture to talk about, not a time sheet.

Try it with the demo team Download (ZIP) Read the guide

Free and open source (MIT). One HTML file: no installation, no account, no server.

Made for research groups

For group leaders, coordinators and everyone in the team. Research projects move from proposal to finished; courses, supervision and meetings sit next to them.

The team at a glance

Home says in one sentence what may be worth a look: someone above their full-plate level, an overdue deadline, a project without people, who has room.

Involvement, not hours

An optional percentage per assignment, task groups with detailed tasks, and each person's own word on how full their plate is. Weekly hours only if the team wants them.

Projects as they really are

Proposal, submitted, granted, ongoing and finished, with extensions, milestones and deadlines on a timeline. Teaching periods, supervision and recurring meetings too.

Work that crosses projects

Shared work links the tasks that several projects build on, with a map of what waits on what.

Share it your way

Keep the team file in a shared folder: Chrome and Edge save to it and merge colleagues' changes. Export Excel, person cards as PDF, picture or PowerPoint, and timelines.

Private by design

Teams live in your browser and in the files you save. The only network request is the optional weather on Home, which sends just the place you chose.

A closer look

All screenshots show fictional demo teams that come with the app.

Start in a minute

  1. Get the appOpen it online, or download the ZIP and double-click Team capacity.html.
  2. Look aroundIt opens with a fictional lab of ten people. Add a second demo team of 24 from the team switcher.
  3. Create your teamAdd people, projects and activities, then connect them with assignments.
  4. Save and shareSave the team file in a shared folder so everyone can open it.

Chrome, Edge, Arc and Opera can keep saving to a shared file. Safari and Firefox work too, but save each change as a new copy in Downloads.
